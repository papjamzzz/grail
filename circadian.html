How to read your Ring
Your whole day on one clock — midnight at the top, noon at the bottom.
The bright cursor is right now. Behind it is today as you have lived it; ahead of it, dimmed, is yesterday still waiting to be written over. The centre shows the time, your state, and HR & HRV at whatever moment the cursor is on.
Hover any band to read it. Point at the ring and a card names that band, shows what it reads at that hour, and says what it means. That is the colour key — there is no separate legend to memorise.
One rule, everywhere on this ring: green means real. A green reading is an actual Apple Health value, tagged LIVE (under 10 minutes old) or RECORDED (older, still real — it holds until a newer one replaces it, no cutoff). White is an estimate — a typical-day pattern, shown only where no real reading exists yet. Every metric is judged on its own: HR can be real while HRV is still an estimate, or the other way around.
Green dots on the HR and HRV bands are your actual synced readings from the last 24 hours — one per sync, not a smooth curve; more frequent syncs make a denser, smoother trail. Dots connect with a line when synced within 10 hours of each other; a wider gap means a sync was missed, and stays a real, honest gap rather than a guessed line.
Drag anywhere on the ring to look back through your day. Let go and the cursor glides home to now.
◐
The ◐ button up top switches views. By default the ring shows the full 24 hours — bright behind the cursor is today as you have lived it, dimmed ahead of it is yesterday, waiting to be written over. Switch it to Today so far to leave the unlived hours blank instead.
The blue arc is sleep. Purple stretches inside it are deep sleep; the cyan dashes just inside are REM.
Hot pink flares are training. Yellow arcs are lighter movement, like a walk.
The red line outside the ring is your heart rate — watch it swell where you trained and sink while you slept.
The cyan line inside is HRV — highest in deep night, dipping after hard effort, rebounding as you recover.
The ring's daytime color is your stress load — green is calm, amber is working, red is a spike worth noticing.
Amber trails are caffeine burning off — each dose fades over ~10 hours. If a trail reaches your sleep arc, that espresso is still in your blood at bedtime.
The lime band just outside the main ring is your eating window — 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM, a 6-hour intermittent fasting window.
